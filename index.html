01 / WHAT IS GETTING IN YOUR WAY?

Start with what is slowing you down.

You do not need technology for technology's sake. You need the expensive friction inside your company to become visible, understandable and fixable. Pick the problem that feels familiar.

01WHEN YOUR PEOPLE ARE DOING WORK SOFTWARE SHOULD DO
↳

Workflow Auditor

If your best people are copying information between systems, chasing approvals, repeating checks or working around processes nobody has questioned for years, you are paying for friction. Workflow Auditor helps you expose it, estimate where change could matter most and turn the result into a practical roadmap instead of another generic “AI opportunity” list.

◇

Universal AI Knowledge Graph

Your company already knows more than any individual employee can hold in their head. The problem is that the knowledge is scattered across documents, inboxes, databases, source code and applications. The Knowledge Graph creates a connected semantic layer so your people and your AI can work with more of the context your business already owns.

03WHEN YOUR AI CAN TALK BUT CANNOT DO
⌁

Enterprise MCP Server

If your AI stops being useful the moment it needs information from a real system or needs to take a controlled action, you have an integration problem. Enterprise MCP gives you a reusable foundation for connecting AI to the tools, services and data your company actually runs on—without rebuilding the same bridge for every assistant.

04WHEN ONE AI ASSISTANT IS NOT ENOUGH
◎

AgentOS

Your real work is not one prompt followed by one answer. It moves between roles, systems, decisions and exceptions. AgentOS gives multiple agents a foundation for shared context, coordination and human approval so you can make automation more capable without making your operation less understandable.

05WHEN YOUR NEXT MOVE HAS TOO MANY CONSEQUENCES TO GUESS
◫

Business Twin

Before you change a process, restructure a team, automate a function or move a KPI, you need to understand what else moves with it. Business Twin lets you model your organisation, workflows, teams, KPIs and scenarios together so “what happens if we do this?” becomes something you can examine before you spend real time, money or political capital.
